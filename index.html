How to get your data:

  1. Go to privacy.apple.com and sign in
  2. Click "Request a copy of your data"
  3. Select "Apple Media Services activity" and submit
  4. Wait for Apple's email (usually 2-7 days)
  5. Download and unzip the file
  6. Upload Apple Music - Play History Daily Tracks.csv here
